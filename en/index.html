Up close

Woven by hand, rope by rope.

Each placemat is woven by hand from natural jute rope, spiralling out from the centre. The coloured rope on the edge gives the set its colour, and the leather tag carries our stamped “my” mark. Being handmade, pieces may vary slightly in size and weave.

      Our story

      FAQ

      Good to know

      Where can I buy?

      All our products are sold in our Trendyol store in Turkey. The buttons on this page take you straight to each product on Trendyol; payment, delivery and returns are handled by Trendyol under its terms. For orders from outside Turkey, please write to us.

      What is in a set?

      One coaster per placemat. The set of 4, for example, has 4 round jute placemats and 4 coasters in the same colour, 8 pieces in all.

      How big are the placemats?

      Cinnamon and Gold placemats are about 36×34 cm, Mustard and Natural about 35 cm; coasters are about 9×9 cm. Being handmade, sizes may vary slightly.

      Can a hot teapot or pan go on the jute?

      Not directly. Put a trivet under hot teapots and pans, and use burning candles on a holder or plate.

      How do I store them?

      Roll them up and keep them in a dry drawer when not in use.
